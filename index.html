About Me

Photography is a way of slowing down and noticing the world around me. I am currently a junior in college studying business administration with a focus on digital marketing. My interest in both business and creativity has allowed me to explore how photography can tell stories, capture moments, and communicate ideas visually.

I enjoy finding beauty in everyday places and capturing moments that people might otherwise overlook. I am always looking for new perspectives and opportunities to grow as a photographer.

This portfolio is a collection of my work and reflects the way I see the world. I hope to continue developing my skills, connecting with others through photography, and exploring opportunities to work on new projects and collaborations.
